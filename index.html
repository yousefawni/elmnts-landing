How it works

Three moves, no zips, no buckles.

01

Carry

A slim folio in grained microfiber leather, 15 mm thick, with a fold-flat loop handle and recessed loops for the strap. The only metal is on the strap hooks.

02

Unfold

The front swings open on a flexible spine and lies flat: laptop on the left, mouse on the right, wool-felt on both sides. Magnets seal it shut again with one hand.

03

Dock

A steel rail hides inside the spine. The phone stand, pen tray and pouch have magnets in their base, so they park anywhere along it and stay put.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is the deposit really refundable?

Yes. Message us and it is returned in full to the card you paid with, within five working days, at any point before your bag ships. No form, no reason needed.

When will I get it?

The founders' batch is planned for Q2 2027. We will email you a progress note about once a month, with photos of the samples as they arrive. If the date moves, you hear it first and can take your deposit back.

What if it never ships?

Every deposit is refunded in full, automatically, with an explanation. We will not keep money for a product that does not exist.

Where is it made?

Designed in Dubai, sewn by a specialist bag factory in Guangzhou, China, and inspected by a third party before it leaves. Materials: microfiber PU leather, wool-blend felt, closed-cell foam, neodymium magnets, spring steel.

Will magnets harm my laptop or cards?

The sealing magnets sit in the edges, outside the laptop footprint, and the rail is passive steel. Chip cards are unaffected; keep old magnetic-stripe cards out of the pouch. Nothing in the bag is stronger than a MacBook's own lid magnets.

Who is behind this?

ELMNTS is a pre-launch project by Yousef Alsheikh, a consultant in Dubai who got tired of unpacking a bag onto a café table. It is not a registered store yet; that is why the deposit is small and refundable.
